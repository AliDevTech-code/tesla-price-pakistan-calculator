Home Charging vs Public Charging

Not all charging is equal. Where you charge plays a big role in your running costs.

Home Charging (Cheapest)

Charging at home is by far the most affordable option. With a standard charger, most EVs only need a few units per night to cover daily driving. This keeps your Electric car charging cost vs petrol Pakistan difference at its maximum.

Public AC Chargers

Public AC chargers are handy but cost more per kWh than home. Still, in most cases they remain cheaper than petrol.

Public DC Fast Chargers

DC fast chargers are great for road trips, but they are the most expensive public option. Even then, they often cost less per 100km than petrol for most EVs.
