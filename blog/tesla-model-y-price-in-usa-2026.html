Tesla Model Y Price in USA - How Much Does It Cost in 2026?

Updated November 2026 • USA Buyer Guide

The Tesla Model Y is the most popular electric SUV in the United States, and for good reason. It offers family-friendly space, strong range and access to Tesla's Supercharger network. But how much does it actually cost in 2026 once you add fees, taxes and financing? This guide breaks down the Tesla Model Y price in USA 2026 with realistic numbers for every trim.

We cover the sticker price, out-the-door cost, federal EV tax credit, state incentives, monthly payments and long-term ownership costs. You can also run your own numbers with our free Tesla Price Calculator US on the home page.

Federal EV Tax Credit for the Model Y

The federal clean vehicle credit can reduce your cost by up to $7,500, but the Model Y must meet battery and mineral sourcing rules, MSRP caps and buyer income limits. Eligibility has changed several times, so confirm the current rules on the IRS website and Tesla's incentive page before ordering.

If you qualify, the credit may be transferred at the point of sale through a participating dealer or claimed on your federal tax return, depending on how you buy. It is not an automatic discount for every buyer, so plan accordingly.

State and Local EV Incentives

Several states add rebates, sales tax exemptions, carpool lane access or utility charging credits on top of the federal credit. Programs in California, Colorado, New Jersey and other states have changed frequently, so check your state energy office for current details. Charging, Insurance and Maintenance Costs

The Model Y costs less to fuel and maintain than a comparable gas SUV, but insurance can be higher than average. Here is a realistic annual estimate for a US driver covering 12,000 miles per year.

  • Home charging: About 3,430 kWh per year at roughly $0.17/kWh, or about $583 annually.
  • Gas equivalent: A 25 MPG SUV at $3.50/gallon would cost about $1,680 per year.
  • Insurance: Roughly $2,200 to $3,200 per year depending on state, driver and coverage.
  • Maintenance: Tires, cabin filters, wiper blades and inspections, often around $500 to $700 per year.
  • Registration: Some states charge extra EV registration fees to replace fuel taxes.

Over five years, fuel savings can be around $5,500 compared with a 25 MPG gas SUV, which helps offset the higher upfront price.

Is the Model Y Worth It in 2026?

For US families who can charge at home, the Model Y remains one of the most practical EVs on the market. The RWD is the value pick, the Long Range AWD is the best all-rounder, and the Performance is for buyers who want more speed. Factor in the federal credit, state incentives, insurance and charging costs before choosing a trim.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Tesla Model Y cost in the USA in 2026?

The Model Y starts around $44,990 for the RWD, $48,990 for the Long Range AWD and $51,490 for the Performance before destination fees, taxes and incentives.

What is the out-the-door price of a Tesla Model Y?

A base Model Y RWD can land around $50,000 to $51,000 out the door in a state with about 7% sales tax and typical registration fees, before applying the federal EV tax credit and any state incentives.

Does the Tesla Model Y qualify for the $7,500 federal EV tax credit?

Eligibility depends on battery sourcing, MSRP caps and buyer income limits. Many Model Y configurations have qualified in recent years, but confirm current IRS rules and Tesla eligibility before ordering.

What is the monthly payment on a Tesla Model Y?

With about $5,000 down, a 60-month loan and a 6.5% APR, a base Model Y RWD can be roughly $730 to $750 per month after federal incentives. Your actual payment depends on taxes, incentives, APR and term.
