Federal EV Tax Credit Status (Ended)

The federal New Clean Vehicle Credit of up to $7,500 and Previously-Owned Clean Vehicle Credit of up to $4,000 ended for vehicles acquired after September 30, 2025. An earlier qualifying acquisition may still be claimed subject to IRS acquisition and placed-in-service requirements and the applicable filing rules, including Form 8936. A new vehicle acquired after September 30, 2025 does not qualify. See the IRS Clean Vehicle Credits guidance and consult a tax professional about an earlier purchase.

State and Local EV Incentives

Several states add rebates, sales tax exemptions, carpool lane access or utility charging credits alongside any separate state or local programs. Programs in California, Colorado, New Jersey and other states have changed frequently, so check your state energy office for current details. Charging, Insurance and Maintenance Costs

The Model Y costs less to fuel and maintain than a comparable gas SUV, but insurance can be higher than average. Here is a realistic annual estimate for a US driver covering 12,000 miles per year.

  • Home charging: About 3,430 kWh per year at roughly $0.17/kWh, or about $583 annually.
  • Gas equivalent: A 25 MPG SUV at $3.50/gallon would cost about $1,680 per year.
  • Insurance: Roughly $2,200 to $3,200 per year depending on state, driver and coverage.
  • Maintenance: Tires, cabin filters, wiper blades and inspections, often around $500 to $700 per year.
  • Registration: Some states charge extra EV registration fees to replace fuel taxes.

Over five years, fuel savings can be around $5,500 compared with a 25 MPG gas SUV, which helps offset the higher upfront price.

Is the Model Y Worth It in 2026?

For US families who can charge at home, the Model Y remains one of the most practical EVs on the market. The RWD is the value pick, the Long Range AWD is the best all-rounder, and the Performance is for buyers who want more speed. Factor in available state and local incentives, insurance and charging costs before choosing a trim.
