Tesla Model S in USA 2026: Production Status, Used Prices & Ownership Costs

Reviewed November 2, 2026 • USA Buyer Guide

Tesla’s US website now states that the Model S is no longer in production. Older new-car MSRP figures for AWD and Plaid should therefore not be presented as current 2026 prices. Pre-owned Model S vehicles may still appear in Tesla inventory and the wider used-car market, with asking prices varying by model year, mileage, condition, options and location. See Tesla’s official Model S page for the current production notice and inventory link.

Can You Buy a New Tesla Model S in 2026?

Tesla’s current US Model S page says the vehicle is no longer in production and directs shoppers to inventory. As a result, there is no current new Model S AWD or Plaid configurator price to quote. Older MSRP or payment figures may be outdated and should not be used as current buying estimates.

How to Estimate the Cost of a Used Model S

Start with the exact vehicle listing price, then add state and local sales tax, title and registration, dealer or documentation fees, and any delivery charges. If you finance, compare the APR, loan term and total interest—not only the monthly payment. A used Model S can vary substantially in price depending on model year, mileage, battery and drive-unit warranty remaining, accident history and included options.

  • Vehicle price: Use the live asking price for the specific car; do not rely on a historical new-car MSRP.
  • Condition: Review vehicle history, inspection information, tires, brakes, suspension and charging equipment.
  • Warranty: Confirm the remaining basic and battery/drive-unit coverage for that VIN.
  • Insurance: Obtain a quote using the exact model year and VIN before purchase.
  • Charging: Estimate home and public charging using your local electricity and Supercharger rates.

Federal EV Tax Credit Status (Ended)

The federal New Clean Vehicle Credit of up to $7,500 and Previously-Owned Clean Vehicle Credit of up to $4,000 ended for vehicles acquired after September 30, 2025. An earlier qualifying acquisition may still be claimed subject to IRS acquisition, placed-in-service and filing requirements, including Form 8936. A vehicle acquired after that date does not qualify for these credits. See the IRS Clean Vehicle Credits guidance.
