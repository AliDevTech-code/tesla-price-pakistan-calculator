Tesla Model 3 Price in USA 2026 - Full Cost Breakdown

Reviewed November 2, 2026 • USA Buyer Guide

The Tesla Model 3 is still the most affordable way to get into a new Tesla in the United States, but the sticker price is only one part of the story. Destination fees, sales tax, registration, financing and EV incentives can move the real number by thousands of dollars. This guide breaks down the Tesla Model 3 price in USA 2026 so you can plan your budget with realistic numbers instead of guesswork.

We cover every trim, show an estimated out-the-door price, explain the current federal EV tax credit status, and estimate monthly payments and ownership costs. You can also run your own numbers with our free Tesla Price Calculator US on the home page.

federal EV tax credit status (Ended)

The federal New Clean Vehicle Credit of up to $7,500 and Previously-Owned Clean Vehicle Credit of up to $4,000 ended for vehicles acquired after September 30, 2025. An earlier qualifying acquisition may still be claimed subject to IRS acquisition and placed-in-service requirements and the applicable filing rules, including Form 8936. A new vehicle acquired after September 30, 2025 does not qualify. See the IRS Clean Vehicle Credits guidance and consult a tax professional about an earlier purchase.

State and Local EV Incentives

Several states add rebates, sales tax exemptions, carpool lane access or utility charging credits alongside any separate state or local programs. Programs in California, Colorado, New Jersey and other states have changed frequently, so check your state energy office for current details. Charging, Insurance and Maintenance Costs

Owning a Model 3 costs less to fuel and maintain than a gas sedan, but insurance can be higher than average. Here is a realistic annual estimate for a US driver covering 12,000 miles per year.

  • Home charging: About 3,000 kWh per year at roughly $0.17/kWh, or about $510 annually.
  • Gas equivalent: A 25 MPG sedan at $3.50/gallon would cost about $1,680 per year.
  • Insurance: Roughly $2,000 to $3,000 per year depending on state, driver and coverage.
  • Maintenance: Tires, cabin filters, wiper blades and inspections, often around $500 to $700 per year.
  • Registration: Some states charge extra EV registration fees to replace fuel taxes.

Over five years, the fuel savings alone can be around $5,800 compared with a 25 MPG gas car, which helps offset the higher upfront price.

Is the Model 3 Worth It in 2026?

For US buyers who can charge at home, the Model 3 remains one of the strongest value propositions in the EV market. The RWD is the cheapest entry point, the Long Range AWD is the best all-rounder, and the Performance is for drivers who want speed. Factor in available state and local incentives, insurance and charging costs before deciding which trim fits your budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Tesla Model 3 cost in the USA in 2026?

The Model 3 starts around $42,490 for the RWD, $47,490 for the Long Range AWD and $54,990 for the Performance before destination fees, taxes and incentives.

What is the out-the-door price of a Tesla Model 3?

A base Model 3 RWD can land around $47,000 to $48,000 out the door in a state with about 7% sales tax and typical registration fees, before any state or local incentives.

Is the federal EV tax credit status still available for new purchases in 2026?

No. The federal new and previously-owned clean vehicle credits ended for vehicles acquired after September 30, 2025. Earlier qualifying acquisitions may still be claimed subject to IRS requirements, including Form 8936. New purchases after that date do not qualify.

What is the monthly payment on a Tesla Model 3?

With about $5,000 down, a 60-month loan and a 6.5% APR, a base Model 3 RWD can be roughly $680 to $700 per month before state or local incentives. Your actual payment depends on taxes, incentives, APR and term.
