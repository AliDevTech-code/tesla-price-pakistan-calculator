Electric Car Charging Cost in Pakistan – Home vs Petrol

Updated October 2026

One of the biggest reasons people consider switching to EVs in Pakistan is the massive saving on fuel. Here's a realistic look at home charging costs.

Home Charging Cost Calculation

Taking the Tesla Model Y as an example:

  • Average electricity rate at home: PKR 50 per unit (kWh)
  • Battery capacity needed for a full charge: ~75 units
  • Full charge cost: 75 x 50 = PKR 3,750
  • Range on full charge: ~400 km
At home: PKR 3,750 for ~400 km of driving.

Home vs Petrol Comparison

For the same 400 km distance, a petrol car in Pakistan typically costs PKR 12,000 – PKR 15,000. Charging at home can be up to 70% cheaper than petrol.

Factors That Affect EV Charging Cost

  • Electricity tariff: Rates vary by slab.
  • Charger type: Home charger is cheapest. Public DC fast chargers cost more.
  • Vehicle model: Smaller EVs need fewer units.

Final Verdict

If you charge your EV at home in Pakistan, running costs are significantly lower than petrol.
