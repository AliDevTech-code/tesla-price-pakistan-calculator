What Affects Tesla Model Y Import Cost in Pakistan

Several factors directly impact the final amount you pay when importing a Tesla Model Y.

  • Vehicle Price (FOB/CIF): Auction or dealer price in the country of origin is the base.
  • Shipping & Insurance: Ocean freight, insurance and handling add to the CIF value.
  • Exchange Rate: Even a small PKR/USD movement can change the total by lakhs.
  • Customs Duty & Taxes: Applicable duties and taxes are calculated on the assessed value.
  • Clearing Costs: Agent fees, examination, port charges and other local costs.
