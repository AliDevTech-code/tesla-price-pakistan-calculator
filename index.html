Federal EV Tax Credit in 2026

The federal clean vehicle credit can reduce your cost by up to $7,500 when the vehicle and buyer meet IRS requirements. Eligibility depends on the battery and mineral sourcing rules, MSRP caps and income limits. Use the checkbox in the calculator to see the difference, then confirm your eligibility with the IRS and Tesla before ordering.

State and Local EV Incentives

Many US states add their own rebates, sales tax exemptions or utility programs. California, Colorado, New Jersey and several other states have offered EV incentives in recent years, but programs change often. Enter your estimated state or local incentive in the calculator to see how it affects your effective price and monthly payment.

  • Check your state energy office or DMV for current rebates.
  • Ask your utility about off-peak EV charging rates.
  • Confirm whether your state charges an annual EV registration fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Tesla cost in the USA in 2026?

The Model 3 starts around $42,490, the Model Y around $44,990, the Model S around $79,990 and the Model X around $84,990 before destination fees, taxes and incentives.

Is the $7,500 federal EV tax credit still available in 2026?

Many Tesla models may still qualify, but eligibility depends on battery sourcing, MSRP caps and income limits. Always confirm current IRS rules before buying.

How is the monthly Tesla payment calculated?

The calculator subtracts incentives and your down payment from the out-the-door price, then applies your APR and loan term to estimate the monthly payment.

Do states offer extra EV incentives?

Several states offer rebates, tax exemptions or HOV access. Enter your estimated state or local incentive amount to see the effect on your price.
