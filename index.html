Federal EV Tax Credit Status (Ended)

The federal New Clean Vehicle Credit of up to $7,500 and Previously-Owned Clean Vehicle Credit of up to $4,000 ended for vehicles acquired after September 30, 2025. An earlier qualifying acquisition may still be claimed subject to IRS acquisition and placed-in-service requirements and the applicable filing rules, including Form 8936. A new vehicle acquired after September 30, 2025 does not qualify. See the IRS Clean Vehicle Credits guidance and consult a tax professional about an earlier purchase.
