About Tesla Price Calculator US

Independent tools and practical research to help US shoppers understand the full cost of owning and charging an electric vehicle.

What this website helps you do

Estimate a Tesla's out-the-door purchase price, explore state and local incentive assumptions, model a monthly loan payment and compare illustrative fuel and electricity costs. The calculator is designed for planning; it is not a quote, lender offer or official Tesla pricing tool.

Our coverage

We publish explainers about Tesla models, purchase costs, insurance, charging, battery repairs, software changes and ownership questions that matter to US drivers. Our guides aim to separate confirmed information from estimates and items that can change by location or vehicle configuration.

How we research and update information

For time-sensitive claims, we prioritize primary sources such as Tesla's US website, government agencies including the IRS and NHTSA, and public energy data. We link to sources where practical, identify estimates as estimates and encourage readers to confirm current prices, eligibility and program rules with the responsible provider before making a decision.

Vehicle prices, destination fees, electricity rates, insurance premiums, tax rules and incentives can change. A figure shown in a guide is not a guarantee that the same amount applies to every buyer or ZIP code.

Calculator methodology

The purchase estimate combines the vehicle price entered by the visitor, destination and other fees, an entered sales-tax rate, any manually entered incentive and the down payment. The monthly payment is an estimate based on the entered APR and loan term. Actual tax treatment, lender fees, registration charges and financing approvals may differ.

Fuel-savings comparisons use the assumptions displayed with the calculator. Your annual mileage, local gasoline and electricity prices, vehicle efficiency, charging losses and driving conditions will change the result.
